About the Golf Day

Naleko Golf Day is a premier networking and impact-driven event that brings together business leaders, partners, and community builders for a day of meaningful connection through sport. Hosted by Naleko, this exclusive golf day is more than a game - it is a platform to build strategic relationships, celebrate excellence, and drive sustainable economic and social impact.

Whether you join us as a golfer, sponsor, or partner, the Naleko Golf Day invites you to be part of a vision where business, leadership, and impact come together.

Frequently Asked Questions

The Naleko Golf Day is a purpose-led business and networking event designed to bring together leaders, partners, and change-makers to build meaningful relationships while supporting economic empowerment, innovation, and sustainable growth. It is where business, values, and impact meet.

Golf creates a unique environment for authentic connection and trust-building. Naleko leverages this platform to foster collaboration, open doors to opportunity, and mobilise resources toward initiatives that create long-term value for businesses and communities.

Attendees include:

  • Business executives and entrepreneurs
  • Industry and government stakeholders
  • Investors, partners, and professionals

All participants share a common desire to build responsibly, lead with purpose, and contribute to sustainable economic progress.

Sponsorship directly supports Naleko’s mission by enabling:

  • Innovation-driven projects
  • Platforms that connect opportunity to capability

Partners are not just sponsors - they become contributors to meaningful outcomes and measurable impact.

Unlike traditional corporate golf days, Naleko’s event is:

  • Purpose-driven rather than transactional
  • Relationship-focused rather than purely promotional
  • Anchored in values, integrity, and long-term impact

Every partnership is intentional and aligned to Naleko’s broader vision.

Sponsors receive visibility through:

  • Branded on-course and event materials

  • Opportunities for authentic engagement rather than passive advertising
