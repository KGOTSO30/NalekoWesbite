Senior Frontend Engineer

Product & Engineering Midrand, Gauteng Hybrid · Full-time

Posted 18 September 2026 · Applications close 9 October 2026

Build products that move people forward

Help us create fast, inclusive digital services used by people across Southern Africa. You’ll shape frontend standards, solve meaningful product problems and make the team around you stronger.

You’ll join a growing Product & Engineering practice that works closely with clients and Naleko’s learning teams. This is a hands-on senior role: you will spend most of your time designing and shipping software, while creating the clarity and standards that help others do their best work.

What you’ll do

  • Lead the architecture and delivery of accessible, high-performing web experiences across client and Naleko platforms.
  • Partner with product designers, backend engineers and stakeholders to turn complex journeys into clear, resilient interfaces.
  • Evolve shared React and TypeScript patterns, testing strategy and our component library.
  • Coach frontend engineers through thoughtful reviews, pairing and practical technical direction.
  • Measure and improve Core Web Vitals, accessibility and cross-device quality throughout delivery.

Required qualifications

  • 6+ years building production web applications, with deep React and TypeScript experience.
  • Strong command of semantic HTML, modern CSS, browser APIs, automated testing and Git-based delivery.
  • Evidence of shipping inclusive interfaces aligned with WCAG 2.2 and working effectively in multidisciplinary teams.
  • Clear written and verbal communication, sound technical judgement and confidence explaining trade-offs.

Preferred skills

  • Experience building or governing design systems at scale.
  • Next.js, Storybook, Playwright, CI/CD and performance monitoring experience.
  • Experience in consulting, financial services, learning technology or other complex service environments.
  • A practical interest in mentoring and strengthening an engineering community of practice.
Don’t tick every box? We value learning range and lived experience. Tell us what you’d bring—we assess potential as thoughtfully as pedigree.
